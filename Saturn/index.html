SATURN

Saturn's rings are not a solid plate. They are made of countless pieces of ice, rock, and dust!

Appearance

Space Crown

Saturn has shiny rings around it.

They look like a beautiful crown in space.

Soft Stripes

Saturn has gentle cloud stripes like soft watercolor lines.

Floating Planet

Saturn is very light for its size.

If there were a giant bathtub, Saturn could float!

Behavior

Fast Spinner

One Saturn day is only about 10.7 hours long.

Long Year

Saturn takes almost 30 Earth years to go around the Sun.

Imagine waiting 30 years for one birthday!

Long Seasons

Saturn is tilted, just like Earth.

So it has seasons, but each one lasts for many years.

Exploration

Ring Mystery

Saturn's rings are not one solid piece.

They are made of countless bits of ice and rock.

Invisible Shield

Saturn has a magnetic field around it, which works like a calm, invisible energy shield.

Explore Plan

Cassini stayed near Saturn for many years and took amazing pictures of Saturn, its rings, and its moons.

Huygens landed on Titan, Saturn's biggest moon, which was the first landing so far away from Earth!
