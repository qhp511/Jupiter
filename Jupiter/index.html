Jupiter

Appearance

Giant Planet

Jupiter is the biggest planet in the Solar System. It is so huge that more than 1,300 Earths could fit inside!

Stripy Clothes

Jupiter has many colorful cloud bands. It looks like it is wearing brown and white striped pajamas.

Big Red Storm

Jupiter has a giant red storm called the Great Red Spot which has been spinning for hundreds of years!

Gas Ball

Jupiter does not have a real solid ground like a huge ball of swirling gas.

Behavior

Fast Spinner

Jupiter spins very fast. One Jupiter day is less than 10 hours long!

Long Year

Jupiter takes about 12 Earth years to go around the Sun. So birthdays would come very slowly there.

Storm Maker

Jupiter's air is full of wild winds and storms like a giant storm machine that never stops.

Squished Ball

Because Jupiter spins so fast, its middle bulges out. It looks a little like a squished ball.

Exploration

Super Shield

Jupiter has a very strong magnetic field like a giant invisible shield around the planet.

Secret Inside

Deep inside Jupiter, there may be a rocky core and a strange ocean of metallic hydrogen. It sounds like something from a space movie!

The Explorer

The Galileo spacecraft flew around Jupiter for many years. Juno is studying Jupiter now and looks inside the planet and checks its super strong magnetic field.
